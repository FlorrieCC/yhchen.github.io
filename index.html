Bio

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. Yu Li. Previously, I studied at the School of Computing and Data Science at The University of Hong Kong. My research interests lie in AI for Science and bioinformatics, with a focus on large language models, AI agents, and generative modeling for biological discovery. I am particularly interested in RNA design and molecular interactions. My earlier work explored wearable computing and privacy-preserving thermal sensing.
